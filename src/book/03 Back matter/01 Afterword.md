# Afterword {.unlisted .unindented .recto}

Thanks for reading! I hope you enjoyed reading this as much as I enjoyed writing this, I've had great fun spending a summer trying something new.

My first encounter with the XZ Utils backdoor was in 2024 when it made headlines. Since then, it has been in the back of my mind. When I saw how many posts and videos have been made about XZ Utils, yet no published books, I saw a gap that I could try fulfil. The timeline of the social engineering that lead to the backdoor lended itself naturally to a story, so I focused on trying to represent the events as faithfully as possible.

I discovered my enjoyment for writing in February 2026 when I started writing blog posts for my site *hawksley.dev*. For my summer holiday, I wanted to tackle a large project - which ended up being this book you just read!
